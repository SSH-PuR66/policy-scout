SAMPLE INSTRUCTION 5200.YY — SOFTWARE SUPPLY CHAIN RISK MANAGEMENT (DEMO TEXT)

1. PURPOSE. Establishes sample policy for software supply chain risk management (SCRM)
across the acquisition and sustainment lifecycle of sample capabilities.

2. POLICY.
a. Programs shall obtain a software bill of materials (SBOM) for delivered software,
maintaining a component inventory sufficient to identify affected products when a new
vulnerability (CVE) is disclosed.
b. Vendors shall attest to secure software development practices consistent with
DevSecOps and the secure software development framework.
c. Continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines shall enforce
static and dynamic security testing, dependency scanning, and signed artifacts before
promotion to production.
d. Third-party and open-source components shall be evaluated for provenance, known
vulnerabilities, and vendor risk before authorization to operate (ATO).
e. Compromise of a supplier shall be treated as a potential intrusion vector and
trigger incident response and re-assessment of affected authorization boundaries.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES. The sample program executive office maintains the SCRM register
and reports supply chain risk posture under the risk management framework.
