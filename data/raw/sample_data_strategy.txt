SAMPLE STRATEGY — DATA, ANALYTICS, AND AI ADOPTION (DEMO TEXT)

1. VISION. Accelerate the adoption of data, analytics, and artificial intelligence to
generate decision advantage across the enterprise, from the boardroom to the
battlefield.

2. GOALS.
a. Invest in interoperable, federated data infrastructure.
b. Advance a robust data, analytics, and AI ecosystem with shared services and
reusable components.
c. Expand digital talent management, including upskilling the workforce in data
science and machine learning.
d. Improve foundational data management: cataloging, governance, quality, and access
controls for enterprise data assets.
e. Deliver capabilities for enterprise business analytics, including executive
performance dashboards and readiness analytics.
f. Strengthen governance and remove policy barriers to responsible AI adoption.

3. RESPONSIBLE AI. All AI capabilities shall be developed and deployed in accordance
with responsible AI principles: responsible, equitable, traceable, reliable, and
governable. Test and evaluation of AI models shall address robustness, resiliency,
explainability, and reproducibility.

4. IMPLEMENTATION. An agile, iterative approach with continuous integration and
continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines, DevSecOps practices, and cloud infrastructure
authorized at appropriate impact levels, including Impact Level 5 (IL5) for
controlled unclassified information (CUI).
