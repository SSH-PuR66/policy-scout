SAMPLE INSTRUCTION 5200.XX — CONTROLLED UNCLASSIFIED INFORMATION (DEMO TEXT)

1. PURPOSE. Establishes sample policy for the designation, marking, safeguarding,
dissemination, decontrol, and destruction of controlled unclassified information (CUI).

2. POLICY.
a. CUI is information the government creates or possesses that requires safeguarding
or dissemination controls pursuant to law, regulation, or government-wide policy.
b. CUI shall be processed only on information systems authorized at the appropriate
impact level. Cloud systems processing CUI require authorization at Impact Level 4
(IL4) or Impact Level 5 (IL5) depending on the sensitivity of the information.
c. Personnel shall complete initial and annual refresher training on CUI handling.
d. CUI transmitted electronically shall be encrypted in transit and at rest using
approved cryptographic mechanisms.
e. Unauthorized disclosure of CUI shall be reported and may result in administrative
or disciplinary action.

3. MARKING. Documents containing CUI shall bear the CUI banner marking, category
markings where required, and dissemination control markings such as limited
dissemination controls.

4. SAFEGUARDING. Access to CUI shall be limited to individuals with a lawful
government purpose. Common access card (CAC) authentication is required for systems
hosting CUI.
