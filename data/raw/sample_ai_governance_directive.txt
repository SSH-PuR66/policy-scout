SAMPLE DIRECTIVE 3000.XX — AUTONOMY IN WEAPON SYSTEMS (DEMO TEXT)

1. PURPOSE. This sample directive establishes policy and assigns responsibilities for
the development and use of autonomous and semi-autonomous functions in weapon systems,
including guidance on human judgment over the use of force.

2. POLICY. It is sample policy that:
a. Autonomous and semi-autonomous weapon systems shall be designed to allow commanders
and operators to exercise appropriate levels of human judgment over the use of force.
b. Systems will go through rigorous hardware and software verification and validation
(V&V) and realistic system developmental and operational test and evaluation (T&E).
c. Training, doctrine, and tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) shall be
established to ensure operators understand the functioning, capabilities, and
limitations of the system's autonomy in realistic operational conditions.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES. The sample senior official for autonomy oversees implementation,
coordinates test and evaluation infrastructure, and reviews systems prior to formal
development and again before fielding.

4. RISK MITIGATION. Design and V&V measures shall minimize the probability and
consequences of failures that could lead to unintended engagements, including
safeties, anti-tamper mechanisms, and human-machine interfaces.
