SAMPLE MANUAL 8530.XX — CYBER INCIDENT RESPONSE (DEMO TEXT)

1. PURPOSE. Establishes sample procedures for detecting, reporting, and responding to
cyber incidents affecting sample information systems, including ransomware and data
spillage events.

2. PROCEDURES.
a. Detection. Security operations centers shall monitor logging and telemetry for
indicators of compromise (IOC) and correlate threat intelligence against observed
activity.
b. Reporting. A suspected cyber incident shall be reported to the sample computer
emergency response team within one hour of detection.
c. Containment. Responders shall contain affected hosts to prevent lateral movement,
preserving audit records and forensic artifacts for later analysis.
d. Eradication and Recovery. Malware shall be removed, exploited vulnerabilities
remediated, and systems restored from known-good backups before reconnection.
e. Spillage. Unauthorized disclosure of classified or controlled unclassified
information onto a lower-authorization system shall trigger sanitization of affected
media in accordance with approved destruction procedures.

3. POST-INCIDENT. A lessons-learned review shall update detection content, response
runbooks, and the threat intelligence knowledge base after every major incident.
