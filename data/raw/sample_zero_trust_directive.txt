SAMPLE DIRECTIVE 8500.XX — ZERO TRUST CYBERSECURITY REFERENCE (DEMO TEXT)

1. PURPOSE. Establishes sample policy for adoption of a zero trust architecture
across sample enterprise information systems, superseding perimeter-centric
assumptions of implicit trust.

2. POLICY.
a. The department shall adopt a zero trust architecture in which no user, device,
or network flow is trusted by default. Access decisions shall be made per-request
and continuously re-evaluated.
b. Identity, credential, and access management (ICAM) shall enforce phishing-resistant
multifactor authentication for all privileged and non-privileged accounts.
c. Systems shall enforce least privilege and separation of duties, granting the
minimum access required by mission need to know.
d. Microsegmentation shall isolate workloads so that compromise of one enclave does
not grant lateral movement to another.
e. Continuous monitoring shall provide ongoing authorization, replacing point-in-time
accreditation with a dynamic security posture assessment.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES. The sample chief information security officer oversees zero trust
implementation, target maturity milestones, and reporting under the risk management
framework (RMF) and NIST 800-53 security controls.

4. REFERENCES. DoDI 8510.01; NIST SP 800-207.
